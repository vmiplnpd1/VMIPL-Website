- [ ] Check scroll-up floating header on every page; check dropdown and mobile menu.
- [ ] Verify the Contact Us factory photo crops acceptably at desktop, tablet and phone sizes.

- [ ] Check leadership names, designations, ISO 9001:2015 only, and fifth capability Shot Blasting terminology.
- [ ] Test keyboard navigation, form labels, contrast and visible focus.
